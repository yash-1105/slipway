## What the specification must contain

1. **Scope.** What is being built, in two or three sentences.
2. **Out of scope.** What a reasonable reader might expect and will not get.
3. **User-visible behaviour.** Each capability as something observable, not as
   an implementation note.
4. **Data.** The entities, their fields, and the constraints that must hold in
   the database.
5. **Interfaces.** Endpoints or screens, with their inputs and outputs.
6. **Acceptance criteria.** Numbered, each one checkable by a test. A criterion
   that cannot fail is not a criterion.
7. **Open questions.** Anything the brief does not settle. Do not resolve these
   by guessing; a human reads this at the spec gate.

Be concrete about what is ambiguous. The gate exists so that a person can catch
a misunderstanding here, where it is cheap.
